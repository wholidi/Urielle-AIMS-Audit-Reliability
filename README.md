# Urielle AIMS Audit Reliability

**When AI Auditors Get It Wrong: Evaluating Evidence Grounding and Unsupported Conclusions in Agentic AI Systems**

An AI assurance reliability research project using the [Urielle ISO/IEC 42001 AIMS Toolkit](https://github.com/wholidi/Urielle-ISO42001-AIMS-Toolkit) as the experimental system.

Developed during the **OdySEA Fall 2026 AI Safety Fellowship — Technical Project Track**.

### Research objective

Evaluate whether the existing Clause 4.3 AIMS assessment workflow can distinguish evidence-supported conclusions from unsupported, contradictory, insufficient, or irrelevant evidence.

The study compares the unchanged baseline with one targeted evidence-consistency verification improvement.

### Research milestones

- **Week 1:** Research design and synthetic benchmark validation — completed.
- **Week 2:** Baseline evaluation — planned.
- **Week 3:** Controlled verification intervention — planned.
- **Week 4:** Analysis, reproducibility and publication — planned.

### Research limitations

The benchmark consists of synthetic organizational evidence. Reference judgments were established through research-lead calibration rather than independent blinded human validation.

The findings are exploratory and do not establish general reliability across AI auditors or full ISO/IEC 42001 conformity.
